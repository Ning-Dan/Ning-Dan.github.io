# Third-party course sources and content boundaries

The software in this repository is covered by the root `LICENSE`. Course notes, recordings, slides, images, papers, and assignment materials linked from the learning pages retain their original authorship and terms.

## MIT 6.S184 / 6.S975: Flow Matching and Diffusion Models

The Chinese course at `/learning/diffusion-flow/` is adapted from Peter Holderrieth and Ezra Erives, *Introduction to Flow Matching and Diffusion Models*, and the MIT IAP 2026 course materials at <https://diffusion.csail.mit.edu/2026/>.

The course website states that its content is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA. This adaptation attributes the source, is intended for non-commercial educational use, and should be shared under the same terms. The separate `eje24/iap-diffusion-labs` repository identifies its code license as MIT. Images or excerpts credited inside the original slides to other authors may have separate terms; this site links to those slides instead of relicensing the credited assets.

The lesson text embeds a small number of cited pages from the official slide PDFs for study. It does not mirror complete decks. Full slides, recordings, notebooks, and solutions remain linked to their official locations.

## Stanford CS336: Language Modeling from Scratch

The Chinese course at `/learning/cs336/` follows the Spring 2026 schedule at <https://cs336.stanford.edu/>. It links to the official lecture repository, recordings, and assignment repositories.

This is an independent Chinese explanation, not an official Stanford translation. It does not reproduce staff solutions or fill assignment TODOs. The public Assignment 1–4 repositories identify their code as MIT-licensed; that code license is not assumed to cover every lecture PDF, recording, or website page. Students currently enrolled in the course remain responsible for following Stanford's Honor Code and the course AI policy.

Selected figures are loaded from the official lecture repository by their original remote URLs and carry source captions. The files are not copied into this repository; complete lectures and PDFs are linked.

## UC Berkeley CS285 Fall 2019: Deep Reinforcement Learning

The Chinese course at `/learning/cs285/` follows the Fall 2019 schedule at <https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse-fa19/>. The official page says that lecture recordings are provided for personal informational and entertainment purposes. No broad open-content license was found for the complete slide, homework, or starter-code collection.

The site therefore embeds or links the official materials, explains their ideas in newly written Chinese text, and uses independently written small experiments. It does not mirror the full slide decks, reproduce long transcript passages, copy credited images, or publish homework solutions.

Some lessons display a cited page from an official Berkeley PDF through the original course URL. This keeps the page in its original document and provides a direct link; the PDF is not uploaded to this repository.
